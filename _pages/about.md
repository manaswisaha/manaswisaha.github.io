I am a Principal Researcher & Research Manager in the Digital Experiences R&D group at [Accenture Labs](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/about/accenture-labs-index), where I lead the **Human-AI Collaboration Dynamics (HAICD)** research program. My research investigates how people and AI systems develop productive working relationships over time — and how we can measure, support, and improve those dynamics across individuals, teams, and organizations. [Learn more about the HAICD research program details and agendas here](/haicd/).

HAICD is organized around three research agendas. **Situated AI Assistance** focuses on personalized, real-time AI support for knowledge work and physical tasks, producing systems like [StopGap](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3706598.3714262) (real-time LLM jargon explanation, CHI 2025) and [TranSlider](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09969) (AI-driven personalized scientific text, CSCW 2025). **ADAPT-AI** develops theory and measurement infrastructure for understanding human-AI interaction patterns — including behavioral ontologies, appropriate reliance, and calibrated delegation — grounded in HCI, cognitive science, and organizational behavior. **Human-AI Teaming** extends these ideas to collaborative and organizational scales.

Before this work, my research centered on **urban accessibility**. During my PhD, I developed [Project Sidewalk](http://projectsidewalk.org), an interactive tool that reappropriates online street-view imagery to crowdsource sidewalk accessibility assessments — now deployed in 20+ cities worldwide with [over 1M labeled data points](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/project-sidewalk_huge-milestone-reached-project-sidewalk-activity-7320795652009984019-p76x). I earned my PhD in Computer Science & Engineering from the [University of Washington](https://www.cs.washington.edu/) in 2022, advised by [Jon Froehlich](https://jonfroehlich.github.io/) and closely collaborating with [Jeffrey Heer](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~jheer/), supported by the [Google PhD Fellowship 2020 in HCI](https://ai.googleblog.com/2020/10/announcing-2020-google-phd-fellows.html). During my PhD I held research internships at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com/), [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/), and [Autodesk Research](https://www.research.autodesk.com/).
