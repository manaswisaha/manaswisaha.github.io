I am a Research Scientist in the Digital Experiences R&amp;D group at [Accenture Labs](https://www.accenture.com/us-en/about/accenture-labs-index), where I focus on applying **AI** and **XR** to novel business and industrial challenges. My work centers on designing novel human-tech experiences for the **future of the workplace**, leading two key research threads: **AI-driven Real-time Assistance for Knowledge Work** in collaborative settings, and **Audio AR-based physical task guidance**.

My research adopts a **human-centered approach**, exploring how emerging technologies like **AI, AR, and biosensing** can address diverse domains including **accessibility, urban informatics, and sustainability**. My PhD research specifically focused on **urban accessibility**. I developed interactive, data-driven tools, notably [Project Sidewalk](http://projectsidewalk.org) (which reappropriated online street-view imagery to assess sidewalks), to enhance understanding of urban accessibility and empower decision-making for advocacy, urban planning, policymaking, and daily living through data visualization. *Project Sidewalk* is now deployed in 20+ cities around the world, generating [1M+ point datasets](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/project-sidewalk_huge-milestone-reached-project-sidewalk-activity-7320795652009984019-p76x?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAAMsnUYBgodldwnavKc9_VFeSDTMw4DtoOc).

I earned my PhD in Computer Science &amp; Engineering in 2022 from the [Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.cs.washington.edu/) at the [University of Washington, Seattle (UW)](https://www.washington.edu/), advised by [Prof. Jon Froehlich](https://jonfroehlich.github.io/) and closely collaborating with [Prof. Jeffrey Heer](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~jheer/). During PhD, I did several industry research internships at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com/) (with [Tom Jacobs](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tom-jacobs-4492a91/), [David Tompkins](https://research.adobe.com/person/david-tompkins/)), [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/) (with [Meredith Ringel Morris](https://cs.stanford.edu/~merrie/), [Ed Cutrell](https://cutrell.org/), [Alex Fiannaca](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alfianna)), and [Autodesk Research](https://www.research.autodesk.com/) (with [Justin Matejka](https://www.autodesk.com/research/people/justin-matejka/)). My doctoral research was supported by the **[Google PhD Fellowship 2020 in Human Computer Interaction](https://ai.googleblog.com/2020/10/announcing-2020-google-phd-fellows.html)**.

Before UW, I spent the first two years of my PhD in the Computer Science department at the [University of Maryland, College Park (UMD)](http://www.umd.edu/), working in the [Human Computer Interaction Lab (HCIL)](http://www.hcil.umd.edu/). Earlier, as a research scholar at [IIIT-Delhi](http://iiitd.ac.in), I focused on **energy sustainability and smart home research** with [Prof. Amarjeet Singh](http://www.iiitd.edu.in/~amarjeet/) in the [Mobile and Ubiquitous Computing (MUC)](http://muc.iiitd.edu.in/muc/) group, collaborating with [Prof. Yuvraj Agarwal (CMU)](http://www.synergylabs.org/yuvraj/) and [Prof. Anind Dey (UW, then CMU)](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~anind/).
